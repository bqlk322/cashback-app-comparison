## The fine print: pending periods, tracking failures, and the 6-month rule

This is where cashback app reviews tend to go quiet, and it's where the real user experience lives.

**Your cashback starts as "pending" — and stays there for a while.** After you order, cashback posts as pending while the retailer validates the transaction and waits out the return window. Retailer validation averages one to three months. Travel cashback is slower still: it often won't move to payable until after your stay or trip completes, because hotels can claw back commissions on cancellations. Pending cashback cannot be withdrawn and shouldn't be counted as money you have.

**Tracked orders can still be declined.** Returns, excluded product categories, coupon conflicts, or a prior offer on your account can all void the commission. Reading a merchant's exclusions before checkout takes thirty seconds and prevents most of these disappointments.

**Tracking is your responsibility, not the app's.** To maximize the odds your purchase tracks, the official guidance boils down to: click through from TopCashback immediately before paying, don't touch other coupon sites or portals mid-checkout, disable ad blockers and pop-up blockers, turn third-party cookies on, avoid incognito mode, close other shopping extensions, and keep your order records until the cashback turns payable. If a purchase fails to track anyway, there's a "missing cash back" claims process — but resolving it means waiting for the retailer to confirm the order, which can take months. Patience is part of the deal here; if you need instant answers from customer service, that's a genuine friction point.

**Dormant accounts get disabled.** The terms require logging in at least once every six months, and accounts that go quiet can be terminated, with unclaimed cashback at risk. It's an obscure rule — it lives in the terms of service, not on the signup page — and it's the kind of thing that bites someone who signed up for a big purchase two years ago and never came back. Set yourself a reminder, or just check the site before your next order.

**One account per person.** Multiple accounts, self-referrals, or fraudulent signups can get balances forfeited and related accounts banned.
